**`doctor --fiori --json` no longer reports its pre-login caution as a `warn` check.** The heads-up that a real credential is about to be submitted was recorded as a `login` check with status `warn`, so every successful login came back as two `login` entries — a standing warning beside the passing result — and anything summarising the report (the desktop app's "passed, with notes" banner) read a clean login as a problem. The caution is a terminal heads-up, not a finding: it still prints before the attempt in human output, and `--json` now carries one `login` check, the verdict.
