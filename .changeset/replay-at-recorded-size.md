**A web flow replays at the page size it was recorded at.** A visible recording window fills the screen, but a headless replay used Chrome's small default window, and sites lay out by width: Stadtwerke Flensburg, recorded full screen, showed its "Produkte" menu bar, and every headless replay got the phone layout with the menu folded away and failed there. A single-surface web flow that doesn't pin `browser.viewport` now records the size its page had, and every replay, headed or headless and on any screen, lays the page out at that size. A pinned viewport is kept as written, and traces recorded before this replay as they did.
