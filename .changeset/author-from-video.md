**Draft a flow from a screen recording.** `flowproof author-from-video` (experimental) turns one recording into one draft `.flow.yaml`, the way `author-from-doc` does for a test-case PDF. The video is decoded in the Chrome or Edge web flows already use, so nothing new is installed; the frames where the screen changed (up to 60) go to the configured model in one call, which writes the user's actions in order. The draft never asserts, since a recording shows what happened rather than what should, and anything the frames cannot explain is flagged for `record` to resolve. `--check` reads the video without a model call; `--json` reports the draft's steps in the same shape as `author-from-doc`.
