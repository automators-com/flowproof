**Recorded steps now remember which spec step wrote them.** A trace step had no link back to the line of `steps:` it came from beyond its intent text, so nothing could say which trace steps belong to "step 4" of a flow. Each recorded step now carries an optional `spec_step`, numbered the way a person reads the file: steps inside an `in:` block count one by one, and a `when:` or `repeat:` block counts once, with every step it produced carrying its number. The field is additive; traces recorded before it replay unchanged.
