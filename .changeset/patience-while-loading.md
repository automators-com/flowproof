**A step's element gets more time while its page is visibly still loading.** Replay waits a step's recorded time, usually 5 seconds, for its element to appear. On Stadtwerke Flensburg the order portal showed only a loading spinner for longer than that, and replay gave up on the email field while the page was plainly still loading; recording never noticed, because a model-authored step takes seconds. Past a step's own time, replay now keeps waiting, up to 30 seconds, once it sees a request in flight or a document that has not finished loading, including through a pause between requests, as that portal's spinner had. A quiet page that lacks the element still fails at the step's own time, and the message says when the page kept loading.
