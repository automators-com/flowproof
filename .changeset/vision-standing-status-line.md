**A terminal's status line no longer stops a vision recording as the previous step's failure.** On a host screen whose status line reads `Dokument nicht vollständig` for as long as the document is unfilled, the authoring model reported that line as a problem the previous step left behind, and recording stopped at the next step with advice to add a wait that could never help. On vision, flowproof now remembers what the screen read when each step began; a report quoting text that was already there before the previous step ran is refused and the step is authored instead. Reports of text the previous step did bring still stop the recording, and their hint no longer calls them a loading race: it says the screen still shows a problem and to check that step first (#846).
