**`heal` now applies the proposal you reviewed, and re-records with the flow's values.** The only way to apply a heal was `--apply`, which re-records and applies the new result. Re-authoring can come out differently the second time, so what landed in the trace was not always what the reviewer had looked at. `heal --apply-proposal` copies the existing `.proposed.jsonl` over the trace without touching the app, and the PROPOSED hint now points to it. Heal also ignored the flow's values: it had no `--vars`/`--var` and didn't read the sibling `.values.yaml`, so a flow whose URL or data came from `${VAR}`s could not be healed at all. It now loads the flow the same way `record` does (values, suite context, identity) and takes `--headed`/`--headless`.
