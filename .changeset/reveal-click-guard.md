**A click that reopened a menu while recording no longer closes it on replay.** Recording is slower than replay: on Stadtwerke Flensburg the "Produkte" menu closed by itself while the model authored the next step, so the step reopened it before clicking "Haushaltsstrom". Replay, a second behind, still found the menu open, and the recorded reopening click closed it again, leaving the link under the page. When a click brings the step's next target within reach, recording now gives that click a guard, and replay runs it only while the target still cannot be clicked.
