**A late cookie-consent dialog no longer undoes a step.** On Stadtwerke Flensburg the consent dialog appeared a second after replay's first click, and rejecting it closed the "Produkte" menu that click had just opened, so the next step failed as obscured. Before a step acts on a page whose consent manager is loaded but has not decided yet, it now waits until the dialog has appeared and been rejected. A page without a known consent manager never waits, and no page waits once it is 8 seconds old.
