**Web flows reject cookie-consent dialogs by themselves.** Almost every public site shows one, and a flow had to carry its own step for it, which was wrong more often than not: the dialog loads after the page, appears again on another domain mid-flow, and is missing from recordings and test cases made by someone who had already consented. A web flow now presses a known consent manager's own "reject all" control whenever its dialog appears, on any page and domain, and never accepts. consentmanager.net (including its shadow-DOM dialog), OneTrust and Cookiebot are supported, each checked against a live site; any other dialog is left alone and blocks the flow with the error naming it, as before. `browser: consent: keep` turns it off for a flow that tests the dialog. The trace records which, so traces recorded before this replay exactly as they did. The repair loop no longer writes steps that accept cookies, and reports a blocking consent dialog instead.
