**A web step waits for its page to finish loading before it acts.** A page's own scripts wire up its controls as it loads, and replay acts the moment a button exists. On Stadtwerke Flensburg, clicking the "Produkte" menu while the page was still loading opened it 1 time in 5; once the page had loaded, 5 in 5. Recording never noticed, because a model-authored step takes seconds. Before a step clicks or types on a page that is still loading, it now waits until the page has loaded, at most 10 seconds and once per page.
