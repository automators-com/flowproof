**`app: vision` can double-click and right-click.** Both refused with "not
supported by this driver", so a flow that edits a cell by double-clicking it,
or opens a context menu - common in SAP GUI and grid-heavy apps published
through Citrix - stopped at that step. They now land on the matched text the
way a click does, with the two presses of a double-click well inside the
system's double-click interval.
